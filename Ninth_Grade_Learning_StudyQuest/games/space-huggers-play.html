Space Huggers

Action platformer

Run, jump, and clear each level

Space Huggers is a fast arcade platformer. Move through levels, avoid hazards, and defeat enemies to keep progressing.

How to play

  1. Use the keyboard or on-screen controls to move and jump.
  2. Shoot or interact with objects to open paths and clear enemies.
  3. Reach the end of the level before you run out of health.

Learning tip: Read the on-screen prompts at the start of each level. They explain new moves so you can plan your route instead of rushing in.
