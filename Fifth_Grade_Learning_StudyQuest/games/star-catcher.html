Catch the stars

Use your catcher to collect stars

Move left and right with the arrow keys. Catch as many stars as you can before the timer hits zero.

How to play

  1. Click Start Game to begin a 30-second round.
  2. Press ← and → to move the green catcher at the bottom.
  3. Each star you catch adds 1 point. Your best score is saved on this device.

Learning tip: Watch where stars are falling and move early — waiting until the last second makes catches harder.
