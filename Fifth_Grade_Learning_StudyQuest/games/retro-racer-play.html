Racing challenge

Stay on the track and keep control

Retro Racer is a classic top-down race. Steer smoothly, watch the road ahead, and practice reacting quickly without overcorrecting.

How to play

  1. Press the keys shown in the game to accelerate, brake, and steer.
  2. Stay on the track — going off-road slows you down.
  3. Try to finish laps with steady control instead of sharp zig-zags.

Learning tip: Small steering adjustments beat big swerves. This builds focus and prediction skills you can use in math and reading too.
