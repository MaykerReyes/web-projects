What this app is about

StudyQuest is a practice space for ninth-grade learning. You can work on Algebra, reading analysis, science concepts, and social studies skills, then read a short explanation after each answer so the idea becomes clearer.

1

Pick a subject

Choose All Subjects for a mixed quest, or choose one subject when you want to focus on reading, grammar, science ideas, history, geography, or math.

2

Analyze before you answer

Read the question carefully, identify the key idea, and choose the answer that matches your reasoning. Thinking first helps you learn more from the challenge.

3

Use the explanation

After every answer, StudyQuest shows why the answer works. If you miss one, the explanation is the most important part of the challenge.

4

Track your progress

Correct answers give XP. A streak means correct answers in a row. Progress matters, and missing a question is still part of learning.

What the buttons mean

  • New set: reshuffles a fresh group of questions but keeps XP.
  • Reset: starts over from zero on this browser.
  • Games: opens short focus challenges for quick review.
  • Subjects: lets you switch between Math, Language Arts, Science, and Social Studies.

A good way to practice

  1. Start with one subject for 10 minutes.
  2. Write down any question that feels confusing.
  3. Play one game after the quest as a quick review.
